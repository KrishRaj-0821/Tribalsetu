Deep Dive: Har Stage Ka "Kya Hai", "Kyu Hai", Aur "Kaise Kaam Karta Hai"

1

Stage 1: Smart Onboarding & Upload-First Auto-Fill

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Student ko 50 lambe fields haath se bharne ke bajaye sirf basic documents (Aadhaar + Marksheet) upload karne hote hain. System saara data automatically padh kar form fill kar deta hai.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Tribal students cyber cafe jaakar ₹200 dete hain aur typing mistakes (galat roll no, spelling) ki wajah se 40% forms babu level pe reject ho jaate hain. Ye us dard ko 100% khatam karta hai.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Tesseract / PaddleOCR document se Name, Father Name, DOB, Marks, aur District extract karta hai aur JSON payload bana kar form ke input fields mein populate kar deta hai.

2

Stage 2: Intelligent Scheme Recommendation Engine

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Ek smart recommendation system jo student ki qualification ke aadhar par bataata hai ki uske liye kaunsi MoTA scholarship ya fellowship sabse best aur open hai.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Tribal students ko aksar pata hi nahi hota ki Central Government Oxford/Harvard ke liye 100% free overseas scholarship (NOS) ya Ph.D. ke liye ₹31,000+ monthly fellowship (NFST) de rahi hai.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Decision Tree classifier: Agar Marksheet = 10th $\to$ Pre-Matric. Agar UG/PG $\to$ Post-Matric. Agar Ph.D. enrolled $\to$ NFST. Agar Foreign University Offer Letter detected $\to$ NOS Scheme.

3

Stage 3: Dynamic Document Checklist & Classifier

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Har scheme ke mutabiq dynamic document slots generate karna (jaise College Bonafide, Fee Receipt, Passport, ya Foreign Offer Letter) aur upload kiye gaye document ka type check karna.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Har scheme ki document requirement alag hoti hai. Student galti se Passport slot mein Aadhaar card upload kar dete hain jisse verification mahino delay ho jaati hai.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Backend ka Configurable JSON schema required slots render karta hai. Document Classification model check karta hai: Kya "Passport" slot mein sach mein MRZ code wala passport hai ya nahi.

4

Stage 4: Privacy Guard (DPDP Act 2023) & Preprocessing

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Aadhaar ke pehle 8 digit ko kaala patti lagana (masking) aur rural phone se li gayi blurry, skewed, aur tubelight glare wali photos ko crystal clear flat scan banana.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

12-digit raw Aadhaar save karna kanoonan apradh hai (DPDP Act 2023). Aur Bastar ke laminated certificates par camera flash glare se OCR 80% time fail ho jata tha.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Regex & OpenCV se XXXX-XXXX-1234 masking. Canny Edge + Perspective Warp se image ko seedha karna. Sauvola Adaptive Thresholding se glare hata kar black-and-white banana.

5

Stage 5: Sovereign 5-Pillar Multi-Modal Verification

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Parde ke peeche chalne wale 5 military-grade verification algorithms: ELA Forensics, QR RSA-2048 PKI, Article 342 Gazette DB, Fuzzy Identity Matching, aur NPCI Bank Seeding.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Photoshop se banaye gaye fake caste certificates pakadna, dusre state ke illegal quota claim rokna, aur tribal surname spelling variations (Budhu Ram Munda vs Budhu Munda) solve karna.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

ELA compression error heatmap se tampering pakadta hai. PyCryptodome sarkari digital signature verify karta hai. SQLite MoTA Gazette check karta hai. Token-Sort fuzzy logic spelling match karta hai.

6

Stage 6: Composite Trust Score & Triaging (Green/Yellow/Red)

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Ek mathematical formula jo 0 se 100% score calculate karke application ko 3 transparent channels mein sort karta hai: Green (Auto-Approved), Yellow (Deficiency), Red (Fraud).

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Welfare mindset! Agar bastar ke student ki photo thodi blur hai, hum use reject nahi karte. Yellow channel mein WhatsApp link dekar 15 din mein dobara photo upload karne ka mauka dete hain.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Score = 0.25*Forensics + 0.25*QR + 0.20*Gazette + 0.20*Identity + 0.10*DBT. >=80% = Auto-pass. 50-79% = SMS trigger with 1-click tokenized camera re-upload link. <50% = Officer Fraud Desk.

7

Stage 7: Multi-Factor Merit & Slot Allocation (NOS & NFST)

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

National Overseas Scholarship (NOS) aur Ph.D. Fellowship (NFST) ke limited seats (100-125 slots) ke liye automated, unbiased, corrupt-free merit selection engine.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

10,000 tribal bachhe apply karte hain aur seats sirf 100 hoti hain. Manual selection mein nepotism ya bias ka aarop lagta hai. AI algorithm 100% transparent audit trail deta hai.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Merit = 50% Marks + 30% QS World Ranking Weight + 20% Vulnerability Index. Top 100 ranks ko auto-seat allocation aur remaining ko waiting list number assign hota hai.

8

Stage 8: Post-Selection Fellowship Lifecycle & Supervisor Portal

📌 Ye Kya Hai?

Ph.D. scholars ko 5 saal tak har mahine stipend pahunchane ke liye University Research Supervisor / Guide ka quarterly signoff portal aur direct PFMS DBT release engine.

🎯 Ye Kyu Hai? (Problem Solved)

Fellowship one-time scholarship nahi hoti. Ph.D. scholar college aa raha hai ya research chhod di, ye check kiye bina sarkari fund release nahi ho sakta.

⚙️ Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Logic)

Guide login karke har 3 mahine progress report ko "Satisfactory" approve karta hai. System turant PFMS XML handshake trigger karta hai aur student ke bank account mein stipend release ho jata hai.
