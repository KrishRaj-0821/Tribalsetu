2 Real-World Tribal Challenges vs Sovereign Solutions

1. Camera Glare & Low-Res Mobile Scans

Ground Reality: Rural ST applicants in Bastar or Koraput photograph laminated certificates using budget phones under harsh lights, creating fatal white glare.

TribalSetu Fix: Sauvola Adaptive Thresholding + CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) isolates ink from laminate glare.

2. Tribal Surname Variations Across Docs

Ground Reality: "Budhu Munda" on Aadhaar, "Budhu Ram Munda" on Class 10, and "Budhu Munda (ST)" on Caste doc. Strict equality causes 70% false rejection.

TribalSetu Fix: Dual-Layer Fuzzy Matching (Token Sort + Double Metaphone) cross-triangulated with DOB & Father's Name.

3. Photoshop & Canva Digital Forgery

Ground Reality: Fraudulent candidates alter names or roll numbers on authentic certificates using PDF editors or Photoshop before re-saving.

TribalSetu Fix: Error Level Analysis (ELA) identifies localized compression variance + RSA-2048 State DSC signature validation.

4. Inter-State Migration & Art. 342 Rules

Ground Reality: A caste recognized as Scheduled Tribe in Jharkhand may not be notified as ST in Maharashtra. Exploited for illegal quotas.

TribalSetu Fix: Presidential Order Gazette Database (Art. 342) ensures sub-tribe legality strictly within the issuing State boundary.
